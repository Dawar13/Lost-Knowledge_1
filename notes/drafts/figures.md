Why these three for the main body: Fig 1 makes the mechanism visible in one look and is the figure reviewers will
remember; Fig 3 is the causal evidence (post-training stages); Fig 5 is the consequence that most separates this paper
from Lost in Sampling (sample-based estimates measure the reachable set). If G3b passes, Fig 6 with the self-check
points should replace Fig 5 or Fig 3 in the main body (the decision is the author's).

Things to know before writing around them:

- Fig 2 uses the exact verdict (the stored single-truncation curves are exact by construction), so at top-p 0.9 it
  shows 94.0% for Qwen instruct, while the headline robust RBU is 91.9%. The caption says "exact"; the text must not
  mix the two.
- Fig 2: under top-k, OLMo instruct sits almost on top of the two base models, while under top-p and min-p it is far
  above them. Post-training seems to change how probability mass is concentrated more than the rank of the target
  piece. Worth one sentence in Section 4 or 5 (rank-based truncation is hurt less by post-training for OLMo).
- Fig 1: the cumulative mass of the 7 kept pieces (91.5%) comes from `G0_5090/.../first_token.json`; the reach rows of
  `G0_a3_5090` give 0.909 and 0.911 for the mass above the same piece "Mont" in two different rows (different
  batches). Same prefix, slightly different value: the bf16 batch jitter the numeric margin is there for. A good
  concrete example for the margin appendix.
- Fig 4: the sitelink "quintiles" are unequal (10,574 / 5,855 / 3,725 / 7,255 / 6,028 entities) because sitelink
  counts tie heavily at 2 to 4. The caption says so. Recognition is flat over the first four bins and rises only in
  the top bin, while unreachability falls steadily.
- Fig 5: with the shipped sampler Chao1 equals the observed count (141) and reaches only about 61% of the reachable
  set (231): many reachable entities have tiny probability, so estimators undercount even the reachable set.
  `sampled_outside_reachable` in the estimator JSON is 6 (Qwen shipped), 2 (Qwen top-p 0.9) and 5 (OLMo top-p 0.9):
  entities sampled but outside the template-0 robust reachable set. Probably the ambiguous zone near the boundary,
  not violations (V1 is clean), but whoever writes Section 6 should confirm.
- Fig 6: if G3b's invented-name classes change (scripts/run_p6.sh now points at multilingual classes from
  `mb5.invented_ml`), the hook would draw filtered points under a different definition than the unfiltered points
  from `results/P5/*/frontier.csv`. Re-check the hook and re-inspect the label layout once G3b is final.
