- 2026-11-08 (agent): **Instrument change before G0: numeric margin.** A dense self-check (mb5.selfcheck: score every distinct sequence generate() actually sampled, under the spec that sampled it) failed on 0.5B: 180 of 4,802 sampled sequences scored unreachable (shipped_hf about 8 to 12% of distinct sequences per category, p0.90_T1 about 2%). Two causes, both at truncation boundaries: (a) generate()'s KV-cached logits differ from our batched teacher-forced logits (relative jitter of tokens at or above the target: median 0.0625, q0.99 0.50, q0.999 0.69, max 2.78 raw logits over 6,919 positions; mass-above G differs by up to 0.63 when the target near-ties a high-mass token); (b) bf16 ties, which TopPLogitsWarper orders arbitrarily, so even our own chain cut tokens with mass-above below p (e.g. "Houston", G 0.890 at p 0.9). Exact zero is therefore undefined at the boundary. Decision: classify with a margin epsilon (docs/04 section 2): robustly unreachable = cut even with the target's logit raised by epsilon; headline RBU uses robustly unreachable (a lower bound); exact and upper-bound RBU are reported beside it; V1 counts only robustly unreachable answers that get sampled, and boundary hits are reported separately. analysis.py also prints the verdict the same rules give on exact RBU. epsilon is calibrated on each G0 model's own measured jitter before the gate run (entry below). No G0 data had been seen. Human to review.
